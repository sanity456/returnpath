ReturnCustodyRoute - Four-Role Return Custody Workflow

Notes / Description
Built ReturnCustodyRoute, a reusable intelligent contract. Its GenLayer-native mechanism is semantic category lookup + deterministic window -> ordered four-role custody -> two-image condition-change consensus. Correction: Eligibility and condition-change results are now checked against their exact closed domains inside the validator and again before custody state changes. Inputs and source references are public, caller-supplied, and unverified. It creates no return right, compels no merchant, moves no money, proves no custody fact, and assigns no liability. GenVM lint and strict typecheck pass; 12 direct tests and 1 five-validator integration test pass. StudioNet completed 9 successful isolated-wallet transactions. Its source and full ABI/schema match the pinned evidence exactly. The earlier deployment is superseded.
